# Search Strings

Use these directly in LinkedIn, Indeed, and Google Jobs.

## Software Engineer

1. `"software engineer" ("remote" OR "hybrid") ("automation" OR "internal tools" OR "systems")`
2. `"platform engineer" ("remote" OR "hybrid") ("workflow" OR "integrations" OR "operations")`
3. `"internal tools engineer" ("remote" OR "hybrid")`

## QA Automation / SDET

4. `"qa automation engineer" OR "test automation engineer" OR "sdet" ("remote" OR "hybrid")`
5. `"software engineer in test" ("remote" OR "hybrid") Playwright CI/CD`

## Good optional extras

- Add `100k` or `120k` when a job board supports salary filtering poorly.
- Add `Azure`, `Playwright`, `Power BI`, or `workflow` when you want more role fit.
- Remove `remote` and leave `hybrid` if the remote pool feels too crowded.
