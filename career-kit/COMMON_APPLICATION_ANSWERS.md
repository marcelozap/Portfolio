# Common Application Answers

## Why are you looking for a new role?

I’ve learned a lot in my current role and had meaningful exposure to operational systems, automation, analytics, workflow tooling, and production support. At this point I’m looking for a role with more ownership, stronger long-term growth, and the chance to keep building across systems and software engineering.

## Why this role?

This role stands out because it combines the kind of engineering work I’m strongest in: reliability, automation, workflow improvement, and software that supports real operations. I’m especially interested in teams where ownership, delivery discipline, and practical problem solving matter.

## What kind of roles are you targeting?

I’m primarily targeting software engineering roles, with a secondary focus on QA automation and SDET roles where the work is strongly engineering-oriented.

## Salary expectations

I’m targeting roles in the 100k+ range, depending on scope, team, level, and total compensation.

## Remote / hybrid preference

I’m focused on remote or hybrid opportunities.

## Leadership / mentoring

I’m not positioning myself as a formal people manager, but I do have growing experience leading and mentoring newer teammates, especially around testing discipline, documentation, CI/CD habits, and practical delivery workflows.

## Why should we hire you?

Because I’ve worked on software with real operational consequences. I know how to think about reliability, testing, support, adoption, and delivery discipline, not just implementation. I can contribute as an engineer while also bringing calm ownership and practical communication.

## What makes you different?

I bring a combination of operational systems experience, QA automation discipline, workflow tooling experience, and product range from independent builds. That mix makes me effective in teams that need dependable software, not just isolated code contributions.

## Are you interested in AI roles?

I’m most interested in application and workflow-oriented software roles rather than research-heavy AI roles. My stronger fit is systems, automation, QA, workflow tooling, and product engineering.
