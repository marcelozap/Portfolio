## Best Role Filters

Prioritize roles with:

- software engineer, product engineer, platform engineer, internal tools engineer, SDET, or QA automation titles
- remote or hybrid setup
- 100k+ target range or realistic path toward it
- internal tools, workflow automation, CI/CD, data/reporting, APIs, or product surfaces
- evidence that the team builds software rather than only triaging support tickets

Skip roles that are:

- manual QA only
- support-heavy with little engineering
- ML research or model-training focused
- senior/staff-level without matching scope or pay
- vague contract roles with no product or engineering ownership
