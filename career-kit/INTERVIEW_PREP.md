# Marcelo Interview Prep

Use this to prepare for software engineer, QA automation, SDET, and workflow/internal-tools interviews.

## Core Message

You are a software engineer with 4 years of experience across systems, automation, analytics, workflow tooling, and independent product builds. Your advantage is reliability, automation, practical ownership, and the ability to work in messy real-world environments without overselling the details.

## 60-Second Intro

Hi, I'm Marcelo Zapata. I'm a software engineer with 4 years of experience working across systems, QA automation, analytics, workflow tooling, and practical software delivery. A lot of my work has involved improving reliability, modernizing older workflows, building repeatable validation, and supporting software that people depend on day to day. I've also helped newer teammates with testing, documentation, CI/CD habits, and AI-assisted delivery patterns. Outside of work, I build products like Rally, GateKPT MusicOS, and XIV$ Financial Research OS, which has helped me keep growing on the product and engineering side too. Right now I'm looking for a remote or hybrid role with more ownership and room to grow across systems, automation, and product engineering.

## Shorter Intro

I'm a software engineer with 4 years of experience across automation, systems, analytics, workflow tooling, and practical software delivery. I'm strongest where reliability, ownership, and engineering discipline matter.

## Best Stories To Prepare

### 1. Systems Story

Tell this when asked:

- Tell me about your current role
- What kind of software do you work on?
- What's a meaningful project you've owned?

Answer shape:

At Publix, I work on software and internal tools that support real operational work. That includes modernization work, analytics, workflow tooling, and QA automation. One strong example is helping make validation and release confidence more repeatable on a C#-heavy stack. The work matters because these systems need reliability, regression discipline, rollout confidence, and supportability.

### 2. QA Automation Story

Tell this when asked:

- Tell me about automation work you've done
- How have you improved testing?
- Why are you interested in SDET or QA automation roles?

Answer shape:

One area I'm proud of is leading QA automation for modernization work. I used Playwright and CI/CD workflows to help make validation more repeatable and reliable. What I liked about that work is that it wasn't just writing tests for the sake of coverage. It was about improving release confidence for software with real users and real downstream impact. That's the kind of automation work I enjoy most.

### 3. Ownership / Support Story

Tell this when asked:

- Tell me about a high-pressure situation
- How do you handle production responsibility?
- Have you worked on important internal systems?

Answer shape:

I've worked on software where accuracy, documentation, and careful communication matter. That kind of work teaches you to stay precise and think clearly about impact. In those situations, it's not enough to be clever. You need to understand the system, communicate well, and reduce risk responsibly.

### 4. Mentoring Story

Tell this when asked:

- Have you led anyone?
- Do you mentor teammates?
- How do you work with junior engineers?

Answer shape:

I'm not presenting myself as a formal people manager, but I have helped newer teammates with testing discipline, documentation, CI/CD habits, and practical use of AI-assisted delivery. Before Publix, I taught STEM courses, so helping people ramp up and understand things clearly comes naturally to me.

### 5. Side-Project Story

Tell this when asked:

- What have you built outside work?
- Are you product-oriented?
- How do you learn new systems?

Answer shape:

Outside of work, I build projects like Rally, GateKPT MusicOS, and XIV$ Financial Research OS. They are different systems, but the same pattern runs through them: data, workflow, practice, review, interface design, architecture, and end-to-end ownership.

## If They Ask About AI Helping With Coding

Good answer:

Yes, I use AI as part of my workflow, mostly the same way I'd use documentation, examples, or a strong assistant when I'm exploring options faster. But I still own the work. I review the output carefully, adapt it to the system, debug it, and make sure I can explain the tradeoffs. For me, AI is a speed tool, not a replacement for engineering judgment.

## If They Ask Why You Want To Leave Publix

Good answer:

I've learned a lot there and I've had meaningful exposure to systems, automation, analytics, and supportable delivery. At this point I'm looking for a role with more room to grow, broader ownership, and a stronger path forward in software engineering.
